About

Prototype a data physicalization before printing it. Each cursor movement is scaled by g = 1 − k·v, where v is the printed height as a fraction of the relief (0 at the base, 1 at the top). The same mapping (levels n, curve γ, layer snap) sets the STL, the colours and the friction. Complement uses a second variable for friction (how much of the value is filled in, or how few reads back it) while height still carries the value. This describes cursor motion and printed geometry only, not perception.
