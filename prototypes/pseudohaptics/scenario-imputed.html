Task

Find the cells that look solid but are mostly filled in. Every cell shows a value for 2020, but in some the country reported data in only a few years and the gaps were filled by carrying the last report forward. A cell that feels smooth is mostly measured; a cell that feels sticky is mostly filled in. You are not told how many.

About the data

World Bank World Development Indicators, 48 most populous countries with a World Bank region, grouped by region. 12 indicators. Each cell shows the 2020 value after filling gaps (carrying the last report forward, or the first report backward) and is coloured by its rank among the 48 countries in that column, so high means a high value, not a good one. Friction = the share of the 21 years 2000–2020 with no report: v = missing years ÷ 21; g = 1 − k·v. A grey cell has no report at all in 2000–2020. Cursor motion only, not perception. Nothing is logged.
