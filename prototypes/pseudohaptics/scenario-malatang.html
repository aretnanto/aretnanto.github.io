Task

Mala tang is paid by weight. Build a bowl of about 12 oz with at least one protein and one vegetable, as cheaply as you can. The counter never runs out. You are not told the weights, and size is not weight: a big-looking item can be light and a small one heavy. Pick an item up and feel it. A heavy item drags slowly, a light one moves freely.

Colour = type (price per pound) · size = look only

About

Illustrative weights (ounces) and prices per pound, not market data. One item per grab. Friction applies only while an item is held: v = (weight − lightest) ÷ (heaviest − lightest), g = 1 − k·v. Grab an item in the bowl to take it back out. The bowl is full at about 40 items. Cursor motion only, not perception. Nothing is logged.
