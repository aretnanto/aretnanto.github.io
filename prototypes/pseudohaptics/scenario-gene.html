Task

Find the unreliable rows. A gene whose treated and untreated columns look strongly different may rest on only a handful of sequencing reads. Strong colour that feels smooth is a measured difference; strong colour that feels sticky is probably noise.

About the data

Fruit-fly cell RNA-seq (the pasilla knockdown experiment) from the Bioconductor pasilla package v1.40.0: 4 untreated and 3 treated samples. 40 genes: 18 with the most consistent treated-versus-untreated difference among well-measured genes (mean at least 150 normalised reads) and 22 with the largest raw fold changes among poorly measured genes (mean 3–20 reads). Rows are sorted by raw treated/untreated fold change. Colour = log2 of the size-normalised count, converted to a z-score within the gene. Friction = Poisson uncertainty from the raw read count in that cell: v = 0 at 500 reads or more, v = 1 at 5 reads or fewer, on a log scale; g = 1 − k·v. Cursor motion only, not perception. Nothing is logged.
